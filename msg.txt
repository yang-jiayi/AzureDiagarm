Route around tiles even when the grid is packed tighter than the margin

A 150x75 tile on a 160x85 pitch leaves a 10px gutter, which is narrower than
the 6px clearance the router keeps either side of a line. clearLanes merges
spans within 2*margin of each other, so every column of that grid collapsed
into a single obstacle and no lane was offered at all. countBlocked then
inflated every tile by the same margin, so a route grazing one edge scored
identically to one running the full height of three tiles, and bestDetour
could not tell them apart. The result was an arrow drawn straight through
three tiles it does not connect.

The clearance is a preference, not a requirement: a line 5px from a tile edge
is a far better drawing than a line through it. bestDetour now retries
clearLanes at a degraded margin when the comfortable one yields nothing, feeds
those lanes to routesBetweenSides as crossLanes, and tries shorter stubs
because an 18px stub lands inside the neighbour on a packed grid. A candidate
that crosses no tile interior is accepted only after every candidate has failed
to earn the full margin, so routes that already had clearance keep it.
